# welcome to open prompter

hey. this is a teleprompter for people who write in markdown.

it reads the files in the folder you pick. edit the file on your laptop, the phone catches up within a minute. no paste. no account. no subscription.

tap the middle of the screen to play or pause. slide speed. slide font. tap mirror to flip the text for rig glass.

mirror is free. auto-scroll is free. all of it is free.

when you're ready, swipe back and pick a file from your folder.
